About Our School

Kellem Secondary School is a secondary school located in Dambi Dollo, Oromia, Ethiopia.

Our school is committed to providing quality education and helping students achieve their goals.

Education

Mathematics

Developing students' mathematical knowledge and problem-solving skills.

Science

Learning about science, technology and the world around us.

English

Improving English language skills, communication and writing.

Afaan Oromoo

Learning and developing Afaan Oromoo language and literature.

Dedicated Teachers

Our teachers support students in their academic journey.
